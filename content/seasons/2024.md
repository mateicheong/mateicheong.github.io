<p class="club-lead-intro">The <strong>2024 La Ligue season</strong> was the second season of <a href="/competitions/la-ligue/">La Ligue.</a></p>
</div>


The season ran from April 12 to October 26, 2024. Each team played each other team twice for a total of 306 matches. <a href="/clubs/locomotive/">Locomotive</a> confirmed their first ever championship on October 6, while ending their campaign with a total of 76 points.

Following the inaugural season of La Ligue, many leagues around the world were introduced to a pool of new young players. Contracts still being relatively inexpensive compared to major leagues, many players were sold to european clubs.
