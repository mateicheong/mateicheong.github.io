<p class="club-lead-intro">The <strong>2025 La Ligue season</strong> was the third season of <a href="/competitions/la-ligue/">La Ligue.</a></p>
</div>


The season ran from April 11 to October 25, 2025. Each team played each other team twice for a total of 306 matches. <a href="/clubs/locomotive/">Locomotive</a> won for the second time in a row the league, finishing with a total of 73 points.

During the 2025 transfer windows, the incoming and outgoing transfer records for La Ligue were broken. Aldo Morales from <a href="/clubs/as_drummond/">AS Drummond</a> was sold to Swedish Premier Division club IF Elfsborg for $1M and former <a href="/clubs/levis/">Lévis</a> player Miloš Veličković was exchanged to <a href="/clubs/métabéroutin/">Métabéroutin</a> for a sum of $475K.
