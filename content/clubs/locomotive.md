<p class="club-lead-intro"><strong>Locomotive</strong>, nicknamed Loco, is a professional football club based in Saint-Jérôme, Québec.</p>

The club is two-time La Ligue champions, winning back-to-back titles in 2024 and 2025.
</div>

## History
On July 7, 2022, Locomotive unveiled their identity. Inspired by the Parc Linéaire Le P'tit Train du Nord, a trail that originally was a railway line during the majority of the 90s that runs between Saint-Jérôme and Mont-Laurier. Locomotive's colours are red and black. 

On April 6, 2023, Ilan Mercier was announced as Loco's first Head coach.

During their inaugural season, Locomotive ended 4th in <a href="/competitions/la-ligue/">La Ligue</a> and were runner-up in the <a href="/competitions/coupe_du_québec/">Coupe du Québec</a>. 

In January 2024, the $450K transfer of midfielder Jonathan Poulin to FC Metz in France broke the record for the most expensive sale ever made from a La Ligue team. In February, the club sold Mathys Chambon to Turkish club Galatasaray for $195K, setting the record for the second highest sale. Following a spree of twenty five games unbeaten in a row in the domestic league, Locomotive won the double with their first league title. They followed that accomplishment with the Coupe du Québec trophy in mid October. Locomotive lost a total of only three games across the three competitions they participated in during that year.

The 2025 season had Locomotive participating in five different competitions. This season was the first under new head coach Danny Wilson, who was appointed on December 30 2024, following Ilan Mercier’s departure to MLS side D.C. United at the end of the 2024 season. In April, Locomotive were presented La Supercoupe after a 2-0 win against Métabéroutin. In October, Locomotive were crowned La Ligue champions for a second season in a row. Danny Wilson became the second manager in history to win La Ligue in their first season.

## Stadium
The club plays home matches at Stade Desjardins in Saint-Jérôme, a stadium with a capacity of 7,340.
