### Final
The final was played on October 14, 2025. The winner of the match qualified for the CONCACAF Champions Cup in 2026. The game was played in front of 43,403 fans at the Olympic Stadium in Montréal.

Lévis won their first ever trophy thanks to goals from Janković (37') and Thomas (48'). Laval Nord were not able to score even with a red card coming in for Lévis at the 68th minute.
