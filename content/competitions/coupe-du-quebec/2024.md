### Final
The final was played on October 16, 2024. Meeting again for the second time in a row, Locomotive were looking for revenge after a heartbreaking shootout loss. The winner of the match qualified for the CONCACAF Champions Cup in 2024. The game was played in front of 49,133 fans at the Olympic Stadium in Montréal.

Shortly after an injury to Petite-Bourgogne's Hodžić, forward Rui Santos got the scoring started for Locomotive at the 18th minute. With possesion being largely dominated by Locomotive, they doubled their advance at the 82nd minute with a goal from Joseph Giddings. No shootouts were needed this time for Locomotive to successfuly lock in the club's first trophy.
