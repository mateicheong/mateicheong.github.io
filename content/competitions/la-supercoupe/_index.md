<p class="club-lead-intro"><strong>La Supercoupe</strong> is a trophy contested in an annual match between the champions of <a href="/competitions/la-ligue/">La Ligue</a> and the <a href="/competitions/coupe_du_québec/">Coupe du Québec</a>.</p>
</div>


## History
The Supercoupe is contested at the beginning of the season. Plans are that games are played internationally to attract interest to the league and talent in Québec.
